Postdoctoral Fellow in Mechanical Engineering

Affiliation: Department of Mechanical Engineering

Supervisor: Prof. Dan Zhang

Zhihao Xia works at the intersection of AI for Science and robotics. Zhihao Xia received a Ph.D. in Computer Science and Technology from Fudan University in June 2026.

Current research spans scientific AI agents, large language model post-training, and embodied intelligence, building on work in generalized parallel mechanisms and learning-based mechanism synthesis.
