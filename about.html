About Zhihao Xia

Zhihao Xia is a Postdoctoral Fellow in the Department of Mechanical Engineering at The Hong Kong Polytechnic University, working on AI for Science, scientific agents, and intelligent robotic systems.

Profile

Academic Profile

Zhihao Xia is a Postdoctoral Fellow in the Department of Mechanical Engineering at The Hong Kong Polytechnic University, supervised by Prof. Dan Zhang. Zhihao Xia received a Ph.D. in Computer Science and Technology from Fudan University in June 2026.

Zhihao Xia's current research focuses on AI for Science, including AI agents for scientific research, large language model post-training, domain-specific model training, embodied intelligence, intelligent robotic systems, and robotic manipulation.
